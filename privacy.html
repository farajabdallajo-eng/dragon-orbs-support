Dragon Orbs — Privacy policy / سياسة الخصوصية

Developer: FJ.Developer. Contact: faraj.abdalla.jo@gmail.com.

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to Dragon Orbs Android internal version 0.1.2.

English

Google and AndroidX components support authentication, billing and service operation. Google processes account, purchase, security and technical service information under its own privacy policies. Included billing components provide operational reporting. No claim is made that third-party services process no data simply because the developer has no game server.

Dragon Orbs uses Google sign-in to identify a player. Google supplies an identity credential; the game verifies it and derives an account-specific identifier for local storage. The game does not ask for your Google password, Gmail, Drive, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. The game does not store the raw Google identity token, your email, name or profile picture in its save files.

The developer operates no game server or cloud progress service. Progress, settings, saved rounds, attempts, pass expiry and purchase-token fingerprints are stored on the device. This local save has integrity checks; this is not a promise that every saved value is encrypted or that a modified device cannot tamper with it. Google sign-in does not provide cloud backup or automatic transfer of progress between devices.

Optional time passes use Google Play Billing: US$3 for 12 continuous hours or US$5 for 24 continuous hours, starting when the game first successfully verifies and durably delivers the completed purchase; time awaiting approval does not consume the interval, and restoring an already-delivered purchase does not restart it while the original local activation history remains intact. Clearing data, reinstalling, or using another device can lose that history; there is no server-backed entitlement timeline. These are one-time purchases without automatic renewal. The price and currency shown by Google Play apply at checkout. Google processes payment and related account, purchase and store/security information under its policies. The developer does not receive payment-card details. A successful fresh query that finds no owned purchase suspends the locally cached pass; a network failure alone does not prove revocation. The game queries product and purchase status, verifies receipt signatures, and acknowledges completed deliveries. Expired, acknowledged purchases may be consumed to permit another purchase. Purchase tokens and receipts are processed temporarily; the local duplicate-delivery history stores token fingerprints.

The game contains no advertising SDK or developer-operated gameplay analytics service in the reviewed implementation. Test builds may write local diagnostic logs. Support requests sent by email are received by the developer and email providers; include only what is needed to answer your request, and never send passwords, card details or identity tokens.

Local data remains until deleted through the game's account controls, cleared in Android, or removed with the app, subject to Android behavior. In the game, open Account, sign in to the intended Google profile, choose Delete device profile, read the warning and confirm. This permanently removes that profile's local progress, saved round and access history, and signs it out. It does not delete your Google account, Google Play orders or data on another device, and does not request a refund. Other local profiles are unchanged. Empty ownership metadata and the installation key shared with other profiles remain; they contain no saved progress or raw Google identity. Each device must be handled separately because there is no remote game database. Clearing or losing local data can lose progress and consumed entitlements; do not assume Google sign-in restores them.

For help or deletion of support correspondence, contact faraj.abdalla.jo@gmail.com with the subject “Dragon Orbs — data deletion”. The developer will explain any applicable retention requirement and confirm the request outcome. Do not send your Google password. Google controls data retained in its own account and payment systems; use Google's controls for those records.

Dragon Orbs is intended for ages 13 and above. Privacy notices will be updated when the final app's data practices change. Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/terms.
